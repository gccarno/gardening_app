# Chains

A bicycle chain should be lubricated every 150 to 300 kilometres, or after any ride in the rain. Apply one drop of lubricant to each roller, spin the cranks backwards, then wipe off the excess with a clean rag.

Chains wear and stretch over time. Replace the chain when a chain checker shows 0.5 percent elongation on an 11- or 12-speed drivetrain, or 0.75 percent on older 8-speed and 9-speed drivetrains. Riding a worn chain wears out the cassette and chainrings much faster.
